# 📦 Stockify

### Smarter Inventory Management. Simplified.

Stockify is an inventory management platform designed to help businesses take control of their products, warehouses, and stock operations — all in one place.

The idea behind Stockify is simple: make inventory management easier, more organized, and more reliable.

Whether a business operates from a single warehouse or manages inventory across multiple locations, Stockify aims to provide a centralized solution to track stock, manage products, monitor inventory movements, and keep everything under control.

## ✨ Vision

To build a modern, reliable, and scalable inventory management system that simplifies daily business operations and helps businesses make better decisions.

## 🚀 What to Expect

- 📦 **Product Management** — Organize and manage products in one place.
- 🏬 **Warehouse Management** — Manage multiple warehouses and their inventory.
- 📊 **Inventory Tracking** — Keep track of stock quantities and movements.
- 🔄 **Stock Operations** — Record stock-in, stock-out, and inventory adjustments.
- 👥 **Multi-Company Support** — Allow multiple businesses to manage their data independently.
- 🔐 **Access Control** — Manage users, roles, and permissions.
- 📈 **Insights & Reports** — Gain better visibility into inventory and business activity.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- ⚙️ Laravel
- 🐘 PostgreSQL
- 🅰️ Angular (planned)

## 🚧 Project Status

**Currently in active development.**

Stockify is being built step by step, starting with its backend architecture and core inventory management features.

New features and improvements are being added as development progresses.

Build more than just an inventory tracker — create a complete platform that helps businesses manage their inventory with confidence, clarity, and control.
